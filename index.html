Course syllabus

B.Tech / Diploma

Each unit is 26 modules and takes roughly 10–14 hours. Work through them in order — each builds directly on the one before.

How the units connect. Unit 2 finds the bending moment; Unit 3 turns it into a stress and a section size; Unit 4 checks whether that section is stiff enough. Skipping ahead will leave gaps, because each unit opens by using a result the previous one produced.

What is inside every unit

Virtual laboratory

A working solver, not an animation. Change the span, section or load and every number recomputes.

Worked problems

Twenty solved numericals per unit, graded Easy to GATE, with every line of arithmetic shown.

Seven levels of testing

MCQ, fill-in-the-blanks, true/false, matching, numerical, a case study and an adaptive quiz.

Printable formula sheet

One page per unit, laid out for revision and ready to print or save as PDF.

For students

Your progress and quiz scores are stored in this browser on this device only. They are not sent anywhere, and they will not follow you to another computer or a private window. Export your record from Module 26 if your teacher asks for it.

For teachers

Each unit is one self-contained HTML file. Copy it to a shared drive, a USB stick or the college LMS — no server or installation is needed. Module 26 collects the CSV records students export and reports the class average.

The study helper in each unit is an offline keyword-matched reference, not an AI service. It works without a network connection and sends nothing to any server.

Designed for self-paced, autonomous learning.
